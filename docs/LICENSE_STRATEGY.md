## Scholarly metadata

Software licensing and upstream metadata terms are separate. The [INSPIRE terms of use](https://help.inspirehep.net/knowledge-base/terms-of-use/) describe HEP metadata reuse under CC0 with field-specific restrictions. This application requests a small public-field allowlist: researcher names and identifiers, scholarly affiliations, document titles and dates, author references and roles, publication identifiers, and explicit doctoral evidence. It excludes email addresses, phone numbers, abstracts, keywords, private notes, and full text.

The small saved snapshot and frozen test evidence retain source URLs and their partial-coverage labels. Linked papers and institutional pages keep their own terms; their full text is not redistributed here. HEP Graph is an independent project and does not claim affiliation with INSPIRE.

See [data decisions](DATA_DECISIONS.md) for source documentation and normalization rules. A future software-license choice remains with the project owner.
