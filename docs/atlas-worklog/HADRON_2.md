# HEP Atlas coverage wave: stream hadron-2 (hadron structure, spectroscopy, heavy-ion and parity-violation experiments)

Owner tag `hadron-2`. Worktree `HEP-Graph-ar-c7-hadron`, branch `atlas-c7-hadron`. Verification date of every record: 2026-10-26. Registry rows: `data/atlas/coverage/hadron-2.json`.

## Scope

Amendment A26 (cover all ground-based HEP, including high-energy nuclear physics that shares facilities and methods). This stream covers hadron-structure, spectroscopy, heavy-ion, fixed-target and parity-violation experiments at Jefferson Lab, Brookhaven (RHIC and the EIC), the CERN SPS, HERA, GSI/FAIR, JINR (Dubna), Mainz (MAMI and MESA) and Fermilab (SpinQuest). It owns `topic:hadron-structure` (parent `topic:nuclear-and-heavy-ion`, file `data/atlas/topics/hadron-2.json`). Antimatter, neutron and muon experiments, and electron-positron colliders such as BESIII and KLOE, belong to other streams and are not recorded here.
