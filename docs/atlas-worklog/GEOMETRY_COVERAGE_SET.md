# Local-map coverage set (defined 2026-09-18)

"Major experimental location" for the local-map layer means a facility whose
physical layout matters to understanding its experiments: every accelerator
laboratory (rings, linacs, beamlines, halls), every underground laboratory
(halls/caverns and their depth), every reactor-neutrino site (cores and
detector halls at published baselines), and every observatory whose array or
telescope layout is a scientific fact (string/tank/telescope positions,
array footprint). Site count alone is not the criterion.

Total: 39 facilities in the coverage set, 9 outside it. The coverage report's
"Local map geometry" table is the record of which of the 39 have a map, how
many of their features are to scale, and what remains unlocated. A map with
only unlocated entries is not "covered": the report counts to-scale features.
