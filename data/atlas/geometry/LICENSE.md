# Licence of the geometry records

Geometry records in this directory whose `license.name` is `ODbL-1.0` copy or
derive shapes from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, and are made
available under the Open Database License 1.0
(https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Attribution:
"© OpenStreetMap contributors" (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
Records with `license: null` are the atlas's own derivations from published
figures cited in their `sources`; no third-party geodata is copied into them.
Official laboratory plans, PDFs and imagery are cited, never copied.
