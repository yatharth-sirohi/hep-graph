# Licence of the geometry records

Geometry records in this directory whose `license.name` is `ODbL-1.0` copy or
derive shapes from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, and are made
available under the Open Database License 1.0
(https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Attribution:
"© OpenStreetMap contributors" (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
Records with `license: null` are the atlas's own derivations from published
figures cited in their `sources`; no third-party geodata is copied into them.
Official laboratory plans, PDFs and imagery are cited, never copied.

Records whose `license.name` is not `ODbL-1.0` carry the licence of the open
data their positions were read from, with the attribution string shown under
the map: `CC-BY-SA-4.0` (Pierre Auger Collaboration, Auger Open Data release
2024, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10488964: the station positions of the Auger maps are
an adaptation of that data and stay under the same licence, share-alike),
`CC-BY-4.0` (Telescope Array Collaboration data release on Zenodo, DOI
10.5281/zenodo.8405760: the Telescope Array station records) and `CC-BY-3.0`
(LHAASO Science Book, Chin. Phys. C 46, 030001 (2022): the LHAASO detector and
pool records). Only coordinates (facts) were read from those sources; no table,
image or figure is copied into the repository.
